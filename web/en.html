English entry point

A working record, not a global census.

Zona Gris Atlas records documented events and automatic candidates in critical infrastructure, cyber, airspace, GNSS, rail and civil protection. It does not attribute authorship by inference. The current capture is a small, Europe-heavy sample.

What is live

  • Incident matrix — one row per real event; unknown attribution is shown as to be determined.
  • Map — only records with a documented city or region get a marker.
  • Primary documents — CERT-EU Threat Intelligence, CC BY 4.0, kept separate from incidents.
  • Coverage — query matrix and explicit regional gaps.

Weekly digest

One email a week: documented events, pending candidates and coverage changes. Double opt-in and one-click unsubscribe.

No cookies. No sharing with other projects. What you get.

What it does not do

It does not confirm an attack without evidence, does not infer an actor, and does not fill a missing severity or attribution field by guesswork. A pending record is a candidate, not a confirmed incident.

Coverage gaps

The current automatic capture is concentrated in Europe and uses mostly English queries. There is no equivalent capture for the Americas, Asia, China or Africa yet. Those gaps are declared in the coverage matrix, not hidden.

Data and code

Original data is published under CC BY 4.0; third-party headlines and articles keep their own rights and are linked, not copied. The code is AGPL-3.0. Corrections and source proposals are handled through the repository.
